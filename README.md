Two lines to your past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered. A judge model compares entries two at a time; the sharpest cause and effect takes the $2.

The entry:

> Don't delete the voice message from your father the night he called from the station.
> His voice is the only recording you have of him laughing, and you erased it to save storage.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the two lines (178 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, or to `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set.
